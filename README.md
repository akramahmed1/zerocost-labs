# zerocost-labs

The lab notebook. Reverse-engineering free public data into real products, each in its own repo.

## Products

- **[FirstWin](https://github.com/akramahmed1/firstwin)** - small businesses winning big federal contracts, surfaced from free USAspending.gov data.
- **[planet-hunter](https://github.com/akramahmed1/planet-hunter)** - hunting exoplanet transits in NASA public TESS telescope data.

## Research

`docs/research-notes.md` holds the market and competition analysis behind each product: why these lanes, where the white space is, and what was parked.

## Conventions

- One product per repo. Prototypes land on main once reviewed.
- No secrets anywhere. Public data sources only.
- Prototype scope: prove the pipeline works on real data, show honest limitations, then decide on v2.
