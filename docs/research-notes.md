# Research notes

Why these projects, and where the white space actually is. Updated as prototypes land.

## The thesis
Public data is free. The product is the transformation layer: normalizing messy public records into something a human can use or enjoy. Quiver Quantitative proved this by turning SEC filings and congressional disclosures into a data platform that grew through Reddit content marketing.

## Lane 1: govcon-feed
Competition: GovWin IQ (Deltek), GovSpend, Bloomberg Government (enterprise, sales-led), HigherGov ($500+/yr starter), GovTribe, plus new AI entrants (SAMstream, GovBidWire from $39/mo). The most crowded lane.
White space: nobody owns the free, X-native narrative layer. Wedge: "small businesses that just won their first $1M+ contract" as a feel-good discovery feed. Compounds with the ContractReady MCP connector work.

## Lane 2: exoplanet-hunt
Competition: NASA's open-source ExoMiner++ flagged ~7,000 TESS candidates in Jan 2026; many teams mine the same archives. Academically crowded.
White space: the build-in-public narrative ("solo dev hunts planets live"), plus the Planet Hunters citizen-science path where humans still beat algorithms on long-period planets. A real co-discovery can mean co-authorship, which is publication-grade evidence.

## Lane 3 (parked): satellite change detection
Enterprise-dominated (Planet: $244M FY2025 revenue, $498M backlog; Descartes, Maxar). A solo-dev "$0 satellite anomaly detection" repo already appeared in Oct 2026. Free imagery is 10m resolution vs Planet's daily 3m moat. Parked unless a sharper wedge appears.
